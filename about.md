I am Carlos Mora, a Platform Architect with 25+ years of engineering experience spanning physical on-premise data centers, hybrid enterprise backbones, and multi-cloud ecosystems (AWS and GCP).

Over two and a half decades, I have seen the industry cycle through paradigms, tooling trends, and marketing buzzwords. But under production load, the fundamental laws of engineering never change: **if your system's stability, security, or compliance requires engineers to navigate traps with constant human vigilance, your architecture has failed.**

My work focuses on eliminating those traps. I design platform architectures where safe, auditable, and cost-controlled operations are the default path. Whether governing distributed data platforms across multi-account Snowflake and Databricks, automating Zero Trust interconnects, or enforcing execution boundaries for agentic AI workloads, I build platforms that protect teams from catastrophic errors while unlocking developer velocity.

## Architectural Convictions

- **Deterministic guardrails over human vigilance:** A Slack message, a post-deploy checklist, or a "be careful" warning is an architectural antipattern. If an engineer can trigger an outage by missing a notification on a Friday afternoon, the failure belongs to the system design. Safety must be enforced through code, automated pre-flight gates, and structural IAM constraints.
- **Remove the traps from the floor:** Platform maturity is not about asking teams to walk through minefields more carefully. It is about building abstractions so clean that developers cannot accidentally violate least privilege, leave egress unmonitored, or trigger runaway warehouse costs.
- **Documentation as operational self-care:** Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) and deterministic runbooks are not bureaucracy—they are the primary defense against cognitive overload, team burnout, and human single points of failure.
- **Compliance by construction:** In regulated enterprise environments (HIPAA, SOC 2, HITRUST), compliance cannot be a panicked quarterly audit scramble. It must be an immutable, continuously generated byproduct of platform automation.

## Platform Engineering at Regulated Scale

Operating platforms in regulated environments requires systems designed for auditability, resilience, and strict access boundaries:

- **Regulated multi-cloud scale:** Multi-account data warehouse governance and OpenTofu/Terraform module libraries across AWS and GCP.
- **Safe-by-default tooling:** Administrative tooling executes in preview mode by default. Destructive or privileged modifications require explicit elevation, preventing unintended changes.
- **Compliance by design:** Automated change detection, least-privilege RBAC, and multi-layer cost controls embedded directly into platform infrastructure.
- **Resilience through graceful degradation:** Administrative and audit pipelines tolerate single-account maintenance or transient network partitions without halting broader governance sweeps.
- **Operational documentation:** Two-layer documentation discipline combining clear operator runbooks for engineering teams with structured architectural decision records.
